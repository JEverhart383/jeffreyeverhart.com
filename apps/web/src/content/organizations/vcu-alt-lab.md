 At VCU I worked for 3 years as a web developer and then served briefly as the Associate Director of Learning Innovation. We managed several organizational websites, ran a WordPress multisite called [Rampages](https://rampages.us/) that housed course work, projects, and educational materials across 30,000 subsites. As an organization, we frequently engaged in R&D in educational technology, demonstrating the application of technologies like chat bots, data visualization, AR/VR, geospatial applications, etc. to education at an R1 university. During my period in a leadership role, I heavily supported the universities migration from BlackBoard to the Canvas LMS, and worked across the university to implement an open course payment gateway with Canvas Catalog.
