### [Civil Rights Walking Tour](https://civilrightstour.motonmuseum.org/)

[![](/media/CRWT-Screenshots-1-1024x768.jpg)](https://civilrightstour.motonmuseum.org/)   The Civil Rights Walking Tour is a project sponsored by the Virginia Foundation for the Humanities, Longwood University, and the Farmville Downtown Partnership. I serve as the lead web developer for this project. The CRWT tells the story of the civil rights movement in Prince Edward county during the 1950's. In the wake of desegregation, public school closures propelled a student strike that became a part of the landmark Brown V. Board of Education case in 1954. This is an important story, and I feel honored to help people tell it. V1.0 is a HMTL/CSS/JS application that uses Geolocation to track a users position as they explore a 1.6 mile loop in Downtown Farmville, VA. The app provides contextual information about the locations on the tour. V2.0 will incorporate either an Omeka or WordPress backend so that community historians can more easily add sites and content.
