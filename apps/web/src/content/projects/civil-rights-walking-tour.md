The Civil Rights Walking Tour is a project sponsored by the Virginia Foundation
for the Humanities, Longwood University, and the Farmville Downtown Partnership.
I serve as the lead web developer.

The CRWT tells the story of the civil rights movement in Prince Edward County
during the 1950s. In the wake of desegregation, public school closures propelled
a student strike that became part of the landmark Brown v. Board of Education
case in 1954. This is an important story, and I feel honored to help people tell
it.

V1.0 is an HTML/CSS/JS application that uses geolocation to track a user's
position as they explore a 1.6 mile loop in downtown Farmville, VA, providing
contextual information about the locations on the tour. V2.0 will incorporate
either an Omeka or WordPress backend so community historians can more easily add
sites and content.
