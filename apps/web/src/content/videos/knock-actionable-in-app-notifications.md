Today we’re launching an enhancement to our in-app notifications to support more
actionable types of messages, without needing to drop down into code.
Previously, to add inline actions to your in-app notifications you needed to
customize a feed cell component. With our new in-app actions you can now toggle
between a standard, single-action, or multi-action notification template
directly in the UI.
