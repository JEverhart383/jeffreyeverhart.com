Many companies face challenges with notification systems due to notification
fatigue and rising provider costs. The problem arises when each event triggers a
notification, overwhelming users and inflating bills.

Knock's batching functionality addresses these issues by intelligently grouping
related notifications together. This enhances user experience and reduces
provider costs. The video features a demo comparing three strategies: no
batching, leading debounce, and regular batching. Each strategy is designed to
handle notifications differently.
