In this video, Jeff Everhart and Brad Sickles, the founder and CEO of Nullstone,
dig into some of the philosophies behind modern developer tools, like
flexibility and promoting smooth workflows that meet developers where they are.

As a part of Nullstone's recent GitOps features, they created a Slack
integration that lives in your app's configuration files - powered by Knock -
that alerts you about deployments status and lets you take actions on your
infrastructure from directly within Slack.
