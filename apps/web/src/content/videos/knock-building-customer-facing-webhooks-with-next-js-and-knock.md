Webhooks are a crucial component in the modern SaaS ecosystem, allowing
customers to create integrations without you having to build them yourself. As
you plan your own architecture, it’s important to consider how you’ll secure
your webhooks, make them reliable and fault tolerant, and give developers enough
access to debug their connections.

In this video, I walkthrough and example app using Next.js, shadcn/ui, Knock to
create a self-service webhooks platforms for developers to create, test, and
debug their own webhook connections.
