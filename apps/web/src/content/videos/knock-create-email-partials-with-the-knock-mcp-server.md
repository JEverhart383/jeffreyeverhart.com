Developing modern, responsive emails is hard without the additional burden of
making them reusable and compliant with your design system. Knock partials help
you solve that by allowing you to create reusable template parts across your
messaging channels. With the Knock MCP server, you can speed up your development
time by creating partials from existing html snippets or even product
screenshots.

Knock is infrastructure to send product and customer messaging across channels
like email, SMS, Slack, in-app, and more. In the next video in this series we'll
look at how to create cross-channel workflows.
