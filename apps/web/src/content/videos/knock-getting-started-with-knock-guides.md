If you're using a legacy in-app vendor and a script tag to show your users
in-app messages, there's a better way.

Knock Guides allow you to dynamically power in-app messages like banners,
feature announcements, and upgrade nudges using your own components. This
feature combines the sophisticated targeting logic that marketing and product
teams need, with a developer-first approach that gives you full control over the
behavior and design of your messaging. Now anyone can create and display
messages in their product, without sacrificing performance or compromising on
design like with legacy in-app vendors.
