In this five-minute demo we show how to use Knock to power product and customer
messaging across in-app, email, and Slack.

With Knock, you can:
- create cross-channel workflows using channels like email, SMS, in-app, chat,
push, or webhooks and functions like batching, throttling, branching, fetching,
and delays.
- centralize template management for all your channels and get real-time
previews
- expose preference APIs to your users so they control how/when/where they
receive messages, and Knock respects these automatically
- accelerate your feature development with drop-in components for in-app feeds,
notification inboxes, and Slack/MS Teams connectivity
