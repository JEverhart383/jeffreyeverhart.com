A walkthrough of a side project rather than a tutorial: an Astro starter that
renders a WordPress site's posts and pages from WPGraphQL. Nine minutes, no
build-along — just what's in the repo and why it's arranged that way.

Astro was the newer arrival among headless WordPress front ends at the time, and
the interesting part is how little it asks for. The content stays in WordPress;
Astro does the fetching at build time and ships pages.

Repo: [JEverhart383/astro-wordpress-starter](https://github.com/JEverhart383/astro-wordpress-starter).
