# Batching strategies for high-volume product notifications

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rQXV7_DrAec
Captured: 2026-09-13

---

Many companies face challenges with notification systems due to notification fatigue and rising provider costs. The problem arises when each event triggers a notification, overwhelming users and inflating bills. 

Knock's batching functionality addresses these issues by intelligently grouping related notifications together. This enhances user experience and reduces provider costs. The video features a demo comparing three strategies: no batching, leading debounce, and regular batching. Each strategy is designed to handle notifications differently.
