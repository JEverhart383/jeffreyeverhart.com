# Building alerting features for devtools | Nullstone and Knock

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h3HHZHCSU5E
Captured: 2026-09-13

---

In this video, Jeff Everhart and Brad Sickles, the founder and CEO of Nullstone, dig into some of the philosophies behind modern developer tools, like flexibility and promoting smooth workflows that meet developers where they are. 

As a part of Nullstone's recent GitOps features, they created a Slack integration that lives in your app's configuration files - powered by Knock - that alerts you about deployments status and lets you take actions on your infrastructure from directly within Slack.
