# Building customer-facing webhooks with Next.js and Knock

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tCf6HUgqiNM
Captured: 2026-09-13

---

Webhooks are a crucial component in the modern SaaS ecosystem, allowing customers to create integrations without you having to build them yourself. As you plan your own architecture, it’s important to consider how you’ll secure your webhooks, make them reliable and fault tolerant, and give developers enough access to debug their connections. 

In this video, I walkthrough and example app using Next.js, shadcn/ui, Knock to create a self-service webhooks platforms for developers to create, test, and debug their own webhook connections.
