# Create email partials with the Knock MCP server

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B4dU9LQzcDQ
Captured: 2026-09-13

---

Developing modern, responsive emails is hard without the additional burden of making them reusable and compliant with your design system. Knock partials help you solve that by allowing you to create reusable template parts across your messaging channels. With the Knock MCP server, you can speed up your development time by creating partials from existing html snippets or even product screenshots. 

Knock is infrastructure to send product and customer messaging across channels like email, SMS, Slack, in-app, and more. In the next video in this series we'll look at how to create cross-channel workflows.
