# Send messages on any channel in 5 min with Knock | Email, In-app, Slack, SMS, Push, Teams

Captured: 2026-09-13

In this five-minute demo we show how to use Knock to power product and customer messaging across in-app, email, and Slack.

With Knock, you can: 
- create cross-channel workflows using channels like email, SMS, in-app, chat, push, or webhooks and functions like batching, throttling, branching, fetching, and delays. 
- centralize template management for all your channels and get real-time previews
- expose preference APIs to your users so they control how/when/where they receive messages, and Knock respects these automatically
- accelerate your feature development with drop-in components for in-app feeds, notification inboxes, and Slack/MS Teams connectivity
